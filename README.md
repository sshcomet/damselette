## About

Damselette is a Discord bot named for the quiet figure who hums a lullaby beneath the moon. She keeps a calm, watchful presence over a community: composed in how she speaks, steady in how she works, and always there when the night gets loud.

She was built to be a dependable companion for server teams who want one tidy bot instead of several, with a soft moonlit personality to match.

## What she watches over

| | |
|---|---|
| 🛡️ **Moderation** | Looking after members and keeping a clear record. |
| 🎫 **Tickets** | A quiet desk where members can reach staff. |
| 🌙 **Anti-nuke** | A guard that stands between your server and the worst nights. |
| 🌊 **Raid guard** | Catches floods of new accounts and quiets the server on your say-so. |
| ✨ **Utility** | Small everyday helpers. |
